We hired Matt for our wedding this past September and he did an amazing job. We found him incredibly easy to work with and I feel like we put him to a slight test and he passed with flying colors! For our wedding we didn't want traditional music (we have very eclectic taste), we provided him with a base playlist and he took it and ran with it. He did a great job reading the room and see who was on the floor and what they wanted to hear. Not only were his selections good but his equipment he brings is top notch. The speakers sound so clean and crisp and it had full range of sound from high to low. Standing on the dance floor I could feel the music as if I was at a concert. We like some pretty heavy bass music and his system had no problem pumping out the womps. He also had a full array or lights and effects that made it feel like a night club on that end of the floor. It was amazing. He was even nice enough to provide us with some footage of the reception which was totally an unexpected but very welcomed surprise. If you are getting married and Matt is available I HIGHLY recommend him, he was just absolutely there to help our wedding be great in any way he could and it was more than we dreamt of. Thanks again Matt!!
