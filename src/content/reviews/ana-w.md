We had an amazing experience with The Whidbey Island DJ Collective and DJ Matt Bell.

Matt showed up right on time (early in fact) and came fully prepared with all the equipment and lighting for the tent to ensure the day ran smoothly. From start to finish, he made the entire process incredibly easy and stress-free.

Prior to the wedding weekend, we walked the entire property together to discuss placement, flow and sound logistics since we had the ceremony and the dancing in two desperate locations both of which he covered seemlessly on the day of our wedding. Matt asked all the questions we hadn't thought of ourselves :) even volunteering additional equipment to make logistics easier for the jazz band we hired. He took the time to build a thoughtful playlist that perfectly matched our preferred music styles and wove in song requests from our guests.

The energy was fantastic, and the dance floor was always packed from moment we opened up the floor.

We couldn't have asked for a better DJ to keep the party going and make our special day even more memorable. I would highly recommend Matt and his team for your special event!
