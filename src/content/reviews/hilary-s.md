DJ Lee travelled to our wedding on Orcas Island. He was chill and still arrived with plenty of time when one of the ferries was cancelled. Day of, he was really easy to work with and was on board with last minute changes. We weren't initially going to get a DJ because our wedding was only ~40 people but we have no regrets. We had so much more fun having Lee there and I appreciate that he helped us keep the evening on track (with toasts, ferry announcements etc). He stuck to our playlist and music vibe and kept everything high energy all evening. Highly recommended!
