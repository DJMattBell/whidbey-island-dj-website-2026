**Hi, I'm Matt.**

I'm a DJ and MC on Whidbey Island and I run the Whidbey Island DJ Collective.

I came to DJing through music first. I have a bachelor's degree in music (composition and vocal performance) with a minor in sound engineering, and an MFA in musical theatre writing from NYU. I've spent years on stage in theatre and comedy, and I conduct a community choir. All of it taught me the same thing: pacing, timing, and how to bring a room along with you.

I'm also a teacher. I have a Master of Arts in Teaching with a focus on special education, I've taught music in many different settings, and today I work in an inclusive preschool classroom. My thesis was on classroom acoustics and how sound affects students, so I care a lot about how sound feels to the people hearing it. At your event, that means music that's loud enough to move people and never so harsh that anyone wants to leave. It also means I'm watching who's comfortable, who's checked out, and what the room needs next.

So far I've DJed 52 weddings, 6 auctions, and at least 18 school dances, from barns to gyms to ballrooms.

I live on the island with my wife and our two young kids. They're the reason I do this work the way I do: I want every client treated the way I'd want my own family treated, and I want to be home for the people who matter most. If you're planning something and want a DJ who's glad to be part of it, I'd love to hear about it.
