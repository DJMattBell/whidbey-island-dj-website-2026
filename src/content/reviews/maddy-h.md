Let me paint you a picture.

It's an outdoor wedding. It's our first wedding. We have no wedding planner or any idea what we are doing.

Enter, DJ Matt. With his stellar wedding planning worksheet and all the questions for us we didn't know we had to think of.

DJ Matt was absolutely wonderful to work with. From our first phone call I knew I wanted to book him. He is friendly, down to earth, and has impeccable taste in music. Even if we had had different music tastes, his years of experience in crowd reading will keep the dance floor full all night. Our dance floor after first dance never had less than 20 people on it at a time. He played a last requested song for our group who was very sad when the night came to an end.

In addition to his wonder DJing skills, Matt also provided much needed MC service for us. He made sure the evening ran smoothly, and handled any uncertain moments we had not planned for with poise and grace.

Thanks Matt for being such a a wonderful human being and DJ! You truly made our day so so special.
