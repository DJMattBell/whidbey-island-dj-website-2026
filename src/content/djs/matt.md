Before I was a DJ, I was a special education teacher. That background shapes everything about how I run an event — I pay attention, I adapt, and I make sure everyone in the room feels included.

I started DJing because I love music and I love watching people connect through it. A packed dance floor where the awkward uncle is dancing next to the flower girl? That's the goal every single time.

I bring professional sound, custom lighting, and the kind of preparation that means you never have to worry about the music. You tell me what matters to you, and I make it happen.
